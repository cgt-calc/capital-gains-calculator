hmrc_exchange_rates/ holds HM Revenue & Customs' exchange rates for customs and VAT from April
2002 to April 2016, with the changes HMRC made during a month. Each year's file names the HMRC
pages or files its rates were taken from.

April 2002 to December 2014: HMRC's pages and files kept in the UK Government Web Archive:
https://webarchive.nationalarchives.gov.uk/
Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v2.0:
https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/2/

January 2015 to April 2016: HMRC's own files, listed at
https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/exchange-rates-for-customs-and-vat
Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0:
https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/
